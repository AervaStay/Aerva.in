The Name

Aerva javanica grows where the ground gives nothing back.

It's a wisp of a desert plant — silver, soft, almost weightless — native to the dry hills of Rajasthan, where little else takes root. We borrowed its name because it says plainly what we tried to build: elegance that doesn't need abundance to feel whole.

Every Aerva home follows the same discipline — a lakeside tower in Gahunje, a boho retreat in Pune, a glass cabin above the pines in Jibhi. Different cities, different views, the same quiet standard: nothing added that doesn't earn its place.

"Not more. Just considered."

A Day at Aerva

Loosely kept hours.

Nothing here runs on a schedule but the sun. This is simply how most days tend to settle.

Dawn

Ridge Walk & Wild Breakfast

A guided walk along the old goat trails, ending with tea and millet flatbread cooked over coals on the eastern outcrop.

Midday

The Spring Pool

Fed by an underground spring and kept unheated on purpose — the one part of the retreat left exactly as the hill made it.

Late Afternoon

Stone Bath House

Oil massage and a hammam-style steam room built from local sandstone, drawing on Rajasthani bathing traditions.

Dusk

One Long Table

A single nightly menu, seasonal and mostly grown within the valley, served family-style for all guests who wish to join.
